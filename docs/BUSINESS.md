# Lauren — Business & Roadmap

> Status: Draft (Round 4 of discovery).

## Pricing

| Tier | What you get |
|---|---|
| Free | 2 videos per month, watermarked export |
| Pro | Paid plan with credit packs |
| Premium | Paid plan with credit packs |

AI usage is paid by Lauren and covered by paid plans and credits.

## Source model

Closed source.

## Go-to-market

The founder already has access to creators, who form the first user base and beta group.

## Roadmap

1. **Spike:** footage → Gemini (via OpenRouter) → edit plan JSON → FFmpeg render. No UI.
2. **Prototype:** Tauri app with timeline and rendered preview.
3. **Private beta:** about 10 creators.
4. **Public launch.**

Pace: solo, as time allows. No fixed dates.

## Open questions

- **Payments:** provider and company jurisdiction decided later. If operating from Myanmar, Stripe is unavailable; a merchant-of-record service (e.g. Paddle, Lemon Squeezy) is the likely route.
- **Pro vs. Premium:** what separates the two tiers (credits, export resolution, features).
- **Free-tier cost cap:** limit video length as well as count, so two long videos cannot run up AI cost.
- **Credit unit:** per video, per minute of footage, or per AI request.
